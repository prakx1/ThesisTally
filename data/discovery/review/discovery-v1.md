## Checks performed

- **Research clock and custody.** The four development windows ended in 2020. Commit `fbf15eca292d569c58272217d86dfdbeca29e9d5` bound all seven rules and six validation windows through 20 November 2026. During review I identified that a hash of trades alone left window metadata mutable. The author added whole-receipt and both planned-entry hashes, together with the statistics code, qualification code and thresholds, before registration. The family pins the development results. The validated output SHA-256 is `7e3c3f8c4d7a9aff136fef4f0b584f85ee580e02c3a42f874b2ebae32dcddaee`. Each hypothesis has a proposal followed by a decision and evidence link. [Registration](../../../docs/discovery-families/discovery-v1.json)
- **Reconciliation to immutable transactions.** I opened all 20 historical SQLite journals read-only and verified their hashes. Independently aggregating the original share transactions, fees, and cash receipts reproduced net rupees and net R for all 2,953 pullback, 1,797 RSI-below-10 and 804 RSI-below-5 closed positions with zero difference in net R. Fill dates followed signals. The check included 394 cash receipts and ten trade records carrying a compulsory-action event across the three books. B variants can include the same security event. The shared trading engine, corporate-action module and frozen configuration have no discovery changes. This checks the reuse of historical outcomes under an independently sized book. It does not independently re-adjudicate every original issuer action or estimate results for portfolio trades that require cash or position limits.
- **Universe and predictors.** The source regression reconciled every archived signal's ISIN, symbol and sector to its dated membership snapshot, and checked the signal rankings by strategy-year. I separately recomputed volatility from 20 log returns, liquidity from 20 closing-price times volume observations, and size from dated AMFI full-cap worksheets at 27 instrument-dates in 25 distinct dated archive panels. The values, eligible panel ranks, and missing classifications agreed. Every populated cap date in the counted sample precedes the signal and its usable date is on or before the signal. The price snapshots end on the signal session. Later listings and absent observations cannot populate earlier ranks.
- **Descriptive results.** I independently recalculated all 397 development cells, 352 populated, and all 935 full-history cells, 896 populated. Every count, net mean R, number of distinct signal dates, 90% resampled interval, and exploratory label agreed. Closed, mature, counted samples contain 2,847 pullbacks, 1,175 B trades with RSI below 10 and 515 with RSI below 5. Gate-off cells have zero observations in these source books. The pullback development sample contains 31 counted trades, all in 2020. Both B development samples consist of 2017 trades.
- **Rolling validation.** An independent calculation, without importing discovery statistics, matched all 42 filtered/base year comparisons and all 30 B plan-level coverage bounds. For the prior-stock rule I rebuilt the qualifying ISIN lists in each year from actual settlements and completed holding horizons before January's first session. I reconstructed the pooled sample floor, cash profit factor, net mean, 4,000-draw signal-date intervals and positive-window criteria. Pairing used contemporaneous unfiltered opportunities and actual after-cost fills throughout.
- **Multiplicity.** An independent grouping of the two overlapping trade populations by exchange-calendar blocks reproduced each paired difference, its interval and p-value, and the seven BH-adjusted probabilities. Independent ranks of the 10,000 common sign simulations reproduced a mean 0.25 raw rejections, 0.0417 BH rejections, and a 3.35% chance of one or more BH rejections under the complete null. These are conditional model estimates. The adaptive stock ranks remain fixed in these simulations. The expected number of complete qualifying passes is bounded above by the expected number of BH rejections.
- **Isolation.** The native write-boundary tests exercised SQLite, a config fixture, a tag fixture, and link handling. The reviewed CLI child is confined to the discovery directory for file writes. All 17 selected isolation, statistics, registry, and historical-source tests passed locally on macOS. The A126 tag still resolves to `dc8e45c791c4f8485eaa869a6985f492805039b3`. No paper service command was issued in this audit.

## Limits on interpretation

The prior study exposed aggregate results for this historical period. The later subgroup results were withheld until registration, but a future period would be a stronger independent test. The 2026 validation window ends in November. Several B windows failed the 10% data-block coverage gate, and pullback predates the per-plan blockage census. Missing historical sectors and missing size assignments remain explicit. Older AMFI files have migration-era HTTP modification dates. For some sheets the historical usable date is inferred from the internal workbook date and SEBI's five-calendar-day publication requirement. This does not establish the exact original publication timestamp. None of the seven registered filters uses sector or size.
