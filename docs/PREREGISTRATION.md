## Common design

Use the dated, ISIN-tracked Nifty 200 and its official daily bars. Each signal uses the corporate-action-adjusted history available through that close. Orders use raw executable prices, whole shares, the existing NSE modeled fees and five basis points of adverse slippage. Cash dividends and compulsory settlements use the existing pre-tax convention. Missing or locked upper bands prevent purchases. A published locked lower band or absent tradable quote defers an opening sale. An unknown lower band allows a slipped opening sale, explicitly flagged. Positive volume and a tradable security are required. A verified genuine >20% move can be released using official close and band evidence on the appropriate information clock. For rights, the simulated holder declines subscription and receives no entitlement cash. Translate price levels for the rights effect and journal the terms. Archives and results from Stage 2 retain their original conventions.

Use the existing three-calendar-year training, one-calendar-year rolling test schedule, annual fresh ₹10 lakh accounts, 2017 through the latest completed session on 20 November 2026. The ten planned windows include the final partial year. Training provides history only. No fitting, parameter search or selection of dates. Each book reports its actual observed dates and any censored positions. The comparator is the official Nifty 200 gross total-return index on matching sessions if the archive is complete, otherwise the official price index with the reason disclosed. Benchmark returns begin from the prior close when capital is exposed at the first open. Scheduled window endpoints never justify invented liquidation prices.

The existing allocation gate uses the Nifty 50 above its 200-session EMA, at least 50% breadth above the 50-session EMA and India VIX at or below 22. ## A · Steady momentum

**Hypothesis.** Among liquid index members, a smoother, persistent upward price path can outperform the benchmark after monthly turnover and transaction costs.

1. At each official month-end close, take current members with 20-observation median traded value of at least ₹5 crore using the existing close-times-volume measure. Omit unresolved price quarantines. Require the close at or above its 200-session EMA and no absolute single-session close-to-close return greater than 15% in the most recent 90 returns. Use consecutive observations across the scoring and jump-check horizons.
2. Regress log adjusted close on session number for the trailing 126 closes. The score is `expm1(252 × daily OLS slope) × R²`. Ties break by ascending ISIN. Flat prices have zero score. There is no additional minimum score. The sole A variant changes 126 to 252 in this regression.
3. Plan for 15 slots at 1/15 of decision-close portfolio equity each. Retain eligible holdings through rank 30. Sell holdings outside that band, outside the universe or below EMA200. Fill vacant slots from the highest ranked eligible names. Rebalance retained weights monthly. The allocation gate blocks new purchases and top-ups when it fails. Exits and overweight reductions can still proceed. No other stops, swing targets, concentration filters or drawdown brakes apply to A.
4. Use the next tradable session’s opening quotes, sales first. Budget from the known decision-close equity, calculate quantities at actual slipped execution prices, respect cash including costs, and process purchases in rank order. A missed monthly purchase waits for the next rebalance. Pending exits are tracked. A fresh January account may use the immediately preceding completed December month-end signal. The last incomplete month contributes marked returns but no inferred month-end.
5. Report net and benchmark CAGR, maximum drawdown, annualised daily volatility, zero-risk-free Sharpe, turnover and fee/slippage drag. Use 252 sessions to annualise daily volatility and Sharpe. Link successive fresh-account **returns** for the full walk-forward curve. One-way turnover is half the absolute traded notional divided by prior equity. Show drawdowns, periods of underperformance, cash exposure, blocked orders and corporate-action effects.
6. Qualifies only if after costs A strictly beats the same-date benchmark in at least seven of ten windows, total CAGR is strictly higher, and net maximum drawdown magnitude is at most 1.2 times benchmark maximum drawdown. A missing account period cannot be scored as a win or silently removed.

## B · Dip in steady risers

**Hypothesis.** A short oversold dip in an otherwise smooth, liquid uptrend can recover enough to pay for risk and trading costs.

1. Each completed session, apply A’s **126-close** score and eligibility to that date, including the jump test, EMA200, liquidity and point-in-time membership. Consider ranks 1–40. Require close strictly above EMA100 and Wilder RSI(2) strictly less than 10. The sole B variant lowers that threshold to 5. The RSI uses average upward and downward changes, seeded with the first two changes; a flat series gives 50. Freeze the highest adjusted high in the ten sessions strictly **before** the signal.
2. Take a next-open order under r2 gate, surveillance, results and tradability checks. Use the r2 max-chase test on the raw open versus signal close plus 0.5 signal ATR(14). The initial stop is the **actual slipped fill minus three signal ATRs**. This definition supersedes the old 2.5 ATR pattern-stop ceiling and the old pattern-stop gap veto. Keep the percentage distance check of 3–10% inclusive at the actual fill. Size using its actual initial risk. Use the existing ATR smoothing.
3. A close strictly above the frozen high queues a full sale at the next tradable open. The r2 close-based price-stop convention queues a full next-open sale when the closing stop is breached. The time exit is scheduled for the 15th held trading session, with the entry session counted as one, using the existing pre-scheduled execution convention. A simultaneous eligible stop and exit uses stop priority. No partial 2R sale, trailing stop or no-progress exit. Adjust stored levels for corporate actions in their correct units.
4. The funded book uses r2’s 0.5% risk, eight-position, two-per-sector and 4% aggregate open-risk limits, cash, and drawdown brake. When competing for funding, use A score descending then ISIN. The equal-risk book gives each eligible call its own fixed ₹5,000 risk budget with independent overlapping positions. Eligibility and fills apply to both books. Only this strategy competes for each new funded book. The first daily signal is evaluated at a test-window close.
5. Use Stage 2’s versioned R-based qualification, substituting the registered 15-session holding horizon for cohort maturity. A counted window needs at least 20 mature closes. Require 100 or more counted mature OOS closes, net mean strictly greater than +0.25R, lower 90% date-cluster bootstrap bound strictly greater than zero, net rupee profit factor strictly greater than 1.4, and positive counted mean in at least seven of the ten scheduled windows. Report funded and equal-risk decisions. The equal-risk book measures setup expectancy; a funded deployment would also need funded qualification.
6. Report gross, net and cost R, stops and other exit reasons, favorable/adverse excursions, first +1R among losers, time to stop, deferred exits, exposures, and benchmark comparison on the same observed dates. Preserve counts for signals, skipped entries, censored positions and data gaps.

## C · Range Bouncers

Registered 22 November 2026 before computing C selections, signals or outcomes. This is **one experiment** with one primary target and one prescribed sensitivity. Use the existing ten annual out-of-sample windows, the seven-release certified point-in-time archive, r2 execution and qualification, and the revised opening-band and window-coverage conventions. Historical A/B registrations and the frozen A126 paper account have their own rule and data identities.

1. **Monthly selection.** At the last exchange close of each month, use that date’s Nifty 200 members by ISIN and the existing 20-session median traded-value minimum of ₹5 crore. Use adjusted OHLC on the share basis known at that close, with dated corporate actions. The 250-session total shareholder return from the close 250 sessions earlier through the selection close must be between −15% and +15% inclusive. Reinvest gross cash dividends at ex-date close and account for documented unit changes. Record unknown inputs explicitly. The basket selected at that close can be used for signals at that close and until the next month-end decision. Daily signals always require current index membership.
2. **Separate traversals.** For each of the 250 sessions ending at selection, form that session’s trailing 60-session trading range from the minimum daily low and maximum daily high, including that session. Express its closing price as a fraction of that range. A close in the bottom 20% arms a traversal; a later close in the top 20% completes it. Count another only after a new bottom observation. Start unarmed at the beginning of the 250 sessions. Require at least three completions, using inclusive boundaries. A zero-width range supplies no crossing.
3. **Collapse check.** Fix the reference low before the evaluation window: the minimum daily low in the 250 sessions immediately preceding the 250 being evaluated. Every close in the evaluation window must be at least 90% of that reference low. This requires up to 500 consecutive dated observations. Missing or unreconstructible history is recorded as unknown; it cannot establish membership in either fully classified group.
4. **Entry.** At the daily close require closing range position at or below 15% on the trailing 60 sessions and Wilder RSI(2) strictly below 10. Freeze that day’s low, high, width and ATR(14) in executable price units. The primary stop is the frozen low less one signal ATR. The primary target is low + 0.50 × width. The single sensitivity changes only the target to low + 0.80 × width, the lower boundary of the top fifth. Apply existing r2 signal and actual-fill stop bounds (3–10% and at most 2.5 ATR), gap invalidation, 0.5 ATR max chase, adverse next-open slippage, costs, tradability and entry coverage. Keep the range targets on their original signal basis, translated for subsequent corporate actions. No 2R rebase or minimum reward/risk veto applies to these explicitly specified exits.
5. **Exit and sizing.** A close strictly below the stop or at or above the target triggers a full sale at the next tradable open. Stop takes precedence if applicable. The usual r2 pre-scheduled time exit is on the 20th held trading session, entry session counted as one. Use r2 funded risk, cash, position and sector limits, regime, surveillance, results blackout and drawdown brake. For simultaneous eligible C signals use deeper closing range position first, then ISIN to break ties. Also run equal-risk independent overlapping calls at ₹5,000 initial risk. Window-end positions remain marked and separately identified. The strategy is research-only.
6. **Qualification and persistence.** Use mature closes with the full 20-session horizon. Apply the existing sample floor of 20 per counted window, 100 counted closes overall, mean net R above +0.25, positive 90% signal-date-cluster bootstrap lower bound, net rupee profit factor above 1.4, and positive mean in at least two-thirds of coverage-eligible test windows. Report each book and both targets. For each window compare all range-stock and other classifiable Nifty 200 entry signals using the same daily signal, r2 eligibility, executable next opening, and independent equal-risk treatment. Compare the fraction reaching the frozen target on a held close before a stop or time exit (target on an already pre-scheduled exit session does not replace that sale). Give numerator and denominator, percentage-point differences, sample coverage and pooled results. Only observable full-horizon filled signals contribute to the main rates. Report ineligible, unclassified, no-fill and censored signals separately. A doubtful price path cannot be scored a loss. The persistence comparison is descriptive and does not select a variant or change qualification.
