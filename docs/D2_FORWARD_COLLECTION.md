# Forward quarterly filing collection

The source archive starts on 23 November 2026 in India. The dedicated user service `com.thesistally.results-filings` runs automatically at login and every 30 minutes on the installed Mac. It checks calendar days, including weekends. After sleep or an outage it catches up on uncollected dates and retries failed catalogue and attachment requests. The Mac must be awake and the user session available to observe a filing in real time. Its next poll can recover only the versions that the exchange still serves. [Unattended installer](../scripts/install_results_filings.py)

The dated NSE feeds cover all equity issuers and include quarterly and annual financial results, integrated financial filings and board/results notices. BSE's dated Results feed captures its own announcement clock. BSE also publishes inline XBRL separately. The service discovers BSE issuers from notices and the latest market-wide financial list, and each day joins the current official Nifty 200 and BSE active equity rosters by ISIN. It preserves unresolved or ambiguous BSE codes as unknown. Membership snapshots describe the day observed. A current snapshot cannot backfill membership on a missed date. [NSE source](../src/thesistally/results_forward_nse.py) · [BSE source](../src/thesistally/results_forward_bse.py) · [Roster identity checks](../src/thesistally/results_forward_roster.py)

A persistent queue visits up to 24 due BSE issuer catalogues per poll. It refreshes successful checks daily and retries failed checks after two hours. An issuer stays under observation for 30 days after its latest roster or discovery observation. The first issuer check looks back to service inception. Subsequent checks overlap the previous seven days to capture delayed versions. The NSE, BSE and member queues are independent. A result notice, its later structured filing and its subsequent revisions keep their own clocks.

The service archives the original exchange responses, the original XML or inline XBRL bytes, their SHA-256 hashes and their retrieval times. Each filing retains its accession, source URL, published time and the exact exchange field supplying that time. If the clock or structured link is absent, its absence remains visible. Revised catalogue rows and new content at an observed URL create additional evidence. A later download alone cannot prove that those same bytes were public at an earlier notice time. No broker login, model or order service is involved.

The live SQLite archive is at `~/Library/Application Support/ThesisTally-results/data/fundamentals/forward/filings.sqlite3`. Routine collection needs no terminal window or daily command. Optional inspection from the research worktree uses `.venv/bin/python -B -m thesistally.results_forward --status`. The status includes saved filings and notices, verified XBRLs, issuer backlog, unknown clocks and outstanding failures.
