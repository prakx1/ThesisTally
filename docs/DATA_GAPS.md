The independent raw close-to-prior-close audit covers the candidate tape over the pilot and its warm-up. The strict threshold uses published decimal closes. Three legacy binary-float comparisons had classified exactly −20.00% as greater than 20% in the combined range. Sixty-two have dated corporate-action evidence and 22 have an explicit price quarantine. Inside the pilot the counts are 40 documented and 15 quarantined. A quarantined day retains the genuine NSE quote as source evidence. A move first discovered at the close cannot undo an eligible opening fill earlier that day. If a funded position remains open when an unresolved price break is detected, the paper engine rolls back the entire session and stops pending resolution. It cannot use that range for a stop or target or publish a closing mark. An affected unfunded shadow call becomes unmeasurable. On later dates the detector uses the real prices after the discontinuity and must accumulate a fresh indicator history. The close-break cuts shorten the input on 5,270 constituent-date scans across 15 ISINs. The audit records each cut, ISIN and affected scan date. The shared portfolio path records what happened to each open holding and pending order. [Local session-level preflight](../data/pilot/preflight.json)
