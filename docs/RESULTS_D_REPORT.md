NSE is the primary clock. An earlier BSE release could move the eligible session when its original filing is unavailable. Raw EPS is retained as printed, so changes in units per share can distort the surprise. An ISIN change can also interrupt the thirteen-quarter series for a continuing issuer. Eicher Motors' August 2020 split is one verified example. Late revisions cannot repair an earlier signal. Older scans and first reports that later changed are harder to certify. Requiring thirteen consecutive original EPS figures can therefore favor issuers with consistently readable filings even in a year that passes the 80% four-field coverage threshold.
